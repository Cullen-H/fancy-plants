# Fancy Plants

Fancy Plants is a farming game that runs in the browser. You plant seeds, wait for them to grow, and sell what you harvest. Weather can change your crops while they grow, and some of those changes combine into rarer ones that are worth a lot more. Later on you can put up buildings that help run the farm.

It started as a CodeHS assignment and is being rebuilt one phase at a time. DESIGN.md has the plan and what each phase added.

## Playing

Open `index.html` in a browser. Nothing needs to be installed. Your farm saves in the browser you play in, so each device keeps its own farm.

## Controls

Click a seed in the bar at the bottom, then click an empty plot to plant it. Click a ripe plant to harvest it. Holding the mouse down and dragging plants or harvests a whole row.

The number keys 1 to 9 pick items from the bar, Q picks the shovel, H harvests everything that's ripe, and Esc puts down whatever you're holding. The up and down arrows switch floors, and S opens Settings. You can change these keys, and add keys for more things, under Settings and then Controls.

On a phone, hold it sideways. Tapping a plant shows when it will be ready.

## Testing

Add `?dev` to the end of the address, like `https://cullen-h.github.io/fancy-plants/?dev`, and a red Test tools button shows up in the bottom left corner. It can add coins, jump the farm to any level, ripen every plant, fill the basket, restock the shops, hand out tools and buildings, add GMO Points, replay the Town cutscene, and set the weather or the time of day.

The test tools change your real save. Use a browser or device you don't mind messing up, or use Reset farm in the options when you're done.
